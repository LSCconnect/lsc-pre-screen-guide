Your application · Candidate guide

Prepare for your pre-screen.

If your application progresses and your experience and background align with the opportunity, the next step will be a pre-screen call with one of our consultants. This conversation is an opportunity to learn more about your experience, understand what you are looking for in your next role, and discuss whether the opportunity could be the right fit for you.

The conversation

What might we talk about?

The call helps us understand your experience, what matters to you in your next move, and whether the opportunity could be a good fit. The conversation will be tailored to the role, so not every topic will apply to everyone.

Your current role

Your current position, employer, responsibilities and relevant life sciences experience. We may also ask whether you are in a permanent or contract role.

Your motivation

What has prompted you to explore a move, what you enjoy in your work, and what you would like to find in your next opportunity.

Role and career goals

The type of position you are interested in, the responsibilities you want, and the skills or experience you would like to build on.

Location and flexibility

Where you are based, preferred work locations, commute, onsite or hybrid preferences, and whether relocation could be an option.

Salary or contract rate

Your salary or rate expectations, and any relevant context that helps us understand what you would consider for a move.

Availability and other processes

Your notice period or availability, whether you are interviewing elsewhere, and any timing considerations that could affect a potential move.

A little preparation goes a long way

How to feel ready for the call

You do not need scripted answers. A few minutes of preparation can help you explain your situation clearly and make the conversation more useful for you.

Think about your experience

Be ready to summarise your current role, the projects or systems you work with, and the experience most relevant to the opportunity.

Know what you want next

Consider the type of role, responsibilities, environment and development opportunities you would like. It is fine if some of these are still taking shape.

Have your practical details to hand

It may help to know your notice period or availability, preferred location, salary or rate expectations, and any flexibility around onsite work or relocation.

Be open about your search

If you are speaking with other employers or have interviews underway, sharing the general timing can help us understand your situation and coordinate appropriately.

Practical details

A few details that may come up

Depending on the role and your circumstances, a consultant may also ask about practical requirements that affect availability or eligibility.

Notice period and availability

When you could potentially start, or the notice you would need to give.

Employment type

Whether you are currently permanent or contracting, and whether you are open to contract opportunities if relevant.

Right to work

Any relevant work authorisation or permit requirements. Only share information needed to establish eligibility for the role.

Location and travel

Whether the site location and expected commute are workable for you; driving may be relevant for some roles.

You do not need to send sensitive documents through this guide. If documentation is required later, your consultant will explain the appropriate next steps.
